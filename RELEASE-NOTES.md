# ARTISTRY AI — Release Notes

## Version 1.0.0 (Build 1) — Production Launch Release

Welcome to the official launch of **ARTISTRY AI**!

ARTISTRY AI is the premier digital drawing studio and AI art companion that empowers artists to master foundational drawing, human anatomy, perspective, and lighting through interactive 60 FPS Skia tools and intelligent guidance.

---

### Key Features in Version 1.0.0

#### 1. Zero-Friction Offline Drawing Experience
- **100% Offline Core**: Sketch, paint, blend, and manage multi-layered projects without an internet connection or account requirement.
- **Privacy First**: Your artwork stays safely on your device in sandboxed storage unless you explicitly choose to export or sync.
- **Auto-Save & Project Persistence**: SQLite-backed local project database prevents any accidental data loss.

#### 2. Classical Art Academy
- **Anatomy Academy**: Interactive 8-head human figure construction guides, skeletal proportion charts, muscle group visualizations, and anatomical landmark checks.
- **Perspective Assistant**: Configurable 1-point, 2-point, and 3-point perspective grids with direct touch-drag vanishing points and adjustable horizon line.
- **Shading Studio**: 360-degree interactive orbital light source controller showing core shadow, terminator, cast shadow, and reflected light on 3D reference forms with a 5-value tonal bar.
- **Reference vs. My Drawing**: True split-screen comparison, opacity-adjustable onion-skin overlay, and pixel-difference heatmap to spot inaccuracies.
- **Curated Reference Library**: Categorized library of copyright-free poses, faces, and architectural studies ready for one-tap canvas loading.

#### 3. 16 AI Drawing Intelligence Capabilities
- Real-time **AI Live Line Coach** analyzing stroke confidence and wobble.
- **AI Proportion & Loomis Validator** for facial and figure alignment.
- **AI Shading & Light Evaluator** comparing tonal values against lighting physics.
- **AI Stroke-by-Stroke Playback** allowing artists to export time-lapse creation videos.
- **AI Smart Color Harmony** with complementary, analogous, and triadic color generators.
- **AI Silhouette & Composition Analyzers** (Rule of Thirds, Golden Spiral).
- **AI Pose Estimator** with 33-point skeletal landmark detection.
- **Bilingual Voice Assistant** supporting hands-free canvas commands in English and தமிழ் (Tamil).

#### 4. Dual User Experience: Beginner & Professional Modes
- **Beginner Mode**: Guided step-by-step overlays, proportion tips, and gentle suggestions.
- **Professional Mode**: Clean, streamlined interface prioritizing maximum canvas real estate, precise numerical inputs, and advanced layer blend modes.

#### 5. Pro Monetization & Cloud Ecosystem
- Flexible Google Play subscriptions (Pro Monthly & Pro Yearly) and in-app credit packs.
- Optional Cloud AI processing powered by Google Gemini Vision and diffusion models.
- Enterprise-grade Scrypt authentication with refresh token family rotation.

---

### Minimum Requirements
- Android 7.0 (Nougat, API 24) or higher.
- Compatible with phones, foldables, and tablets (7-inch and 10-inch form factors).
- Touch screen or stylus input (S-Pen, USI, capacitive).
